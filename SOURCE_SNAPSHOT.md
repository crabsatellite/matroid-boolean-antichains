## Prior boundary

It is standard, and explicitly recorded in a 2021 MathOverflow answer by
David Speyer, that the flats spanned by subsets of a matroid basis form a
Boolean sublattice.  This existence fact is not claimed as new here.  The
project target is the inverse classification of maximum Boolean antichains,
the exact count after simplification, the parallel-class-weighted basis
enumerator, and the interval/minor and rank-tight extensions.

Searches on 30 August 2026 for the exact phrases `Boolean antichain` with
`matroid`, `lattice of flats`, and `geometric lattice` found the new source
paper and the older Boolean-sublattice existence observation, but no prior
classification or basis-enumerator decomposition in the source paper's
sense.  This is a dated negative search result, not a permanent novelty
guarantee.
